SPRAY

Made with Adobe XD

Personal Project

The goal of this app is to help people locate graffitis spreading all over the city, to record their existence, and also to help graffiti artists advocate themselves. In the application, people can take pictures of graffitis that they discover and post them onto the app together with the location information. Also, they can remark the location and write comments. The creators can claim their artwork and explain their intentions and ideas.
