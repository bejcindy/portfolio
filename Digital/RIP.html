Walking Simulator

Concept Designer, Programmer

Developed in Unity, Maya, and Logic Pro

Walk in the graveyard and discover stories of the dead

This is a walking simulator where the player can walk around in the graveyard and hear pieces of music when approaching certain graves. The music is about the life of the dead burried in the grave. After hearing all pieces of music, a secret grave will appear and the fireflies will guide the player into the controled character's own grave.
