Features

Solo, Local & Online Co-op

Play alone or team up with a friend through local or online co-op, with shared progression and separate leaderboards for single-player and co-op modes.

Physics & Dark Humor

Physics-driven gameplay creates unpredictable situations, while reactive dialogue comments on player mistakes, obstacles, and discoveries to deliver the game's dark humor.

Obstacles

Each level introduces unique hazards and environmental challenges that require coordination, timing, and creative problem-solving.
