The Carrot

Board Game for Children

Personal Project

This is a game designed for children from 5 to 10. In the game, each player controls a rabbit represented by different colors and the ultimate goal is to get to the carrot. There should be an adult being the judge to keep the game fair. The winner can get the candy/other prizes inside the carrot. Each turn the player roll the dice and moves forward. The dice has +1, +2, home, -1, and stay. After rolling the dice, the player need to draw two cards from the deck and tell the sum of them. If they get the correct answer, they can then either continue to move forward or be not influenced by the bad ones like “home” and -1.
