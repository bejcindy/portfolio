Jing Fan

Hi! I'm Jing Fan, a game designer and developer with 8 years of experience creating narrative-driven and systems-based games. Co-Founder, Game Designer, and Lead developer at BROKENCIGS, with experience shipping The Hilltop Funeral, releasing Steam Demo for Inkression, and showcasing games internationally. Background in UI design and data visualization, with a focus on intuitive player experiences and gameplay systems.

I hold an M.F.A. in Game Design from NYU Game Center and a B.F.A. in Design and Technology from Parsons School of Design, with a minor in Data Visualization. I am particularly interested in exploring everyday experiences through humor, metaphor, and environmental storytelling.
